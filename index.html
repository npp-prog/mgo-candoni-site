What this office handles

Core functions

Disbursement & Vouchers

Processing of disbursement vouchers, payroll, and payments for municipal offices and personnel.

Cash Advance Monitoring

Tracking cash advances issued to employees and their liquidation status.

Bookkeeping & Reconciliation

Maintaining the municipality's books of accounts and reconciling bank and cash records.

Compliance Reporting

Preparing financial reports in line with COA and DBM requirements.

About this office

Who we are

The Municipal Accounting Office of Candoni maintains the municipality's books of accounts, processes payments, and monitors cash advances for all municipal offices and personnel.

This site is meant to make that work easier to follow from the outside: the forms you need, the checklist for what to attach, the guidelines we follow, and the tools we use internally.

All offices Forms and checklists apply municipality-wide
COA & DBM aligned Guidelines follow national government issuances
